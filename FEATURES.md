## Product In One Sentence

OpenShop is an AI operator for Shopify merchants: a chat-first desktop and cloud agent that can read the store, create content, run workflows, analyze performance, and make approved changes through real Shopify tools.

## Core Positioning

Your Shopify store, on autopilot.

OpenShop is not another chatbot and not another rented SaaS dashboard. It is the layer between a merchant's Shopify store and the AI runtime they already want to use: Claude, Codex, Gemini, ChatGPT, or any MCP-compatible client.

The merchant talks to the store in plain English. OpenShop uses real Shopify tools, skills, memory, and approvals to turn that request into finished work.

## The Customer

Primary users:

- Everyday Shopify merchants
- Small DTC operators
- Solo founders and lean teams
- Growth-minded sellers who care about ads, UGC, creative, ROAS, and store speed

Their context:

- They are time-poor.
- They are not designers or engineers.
- They already run a real store every day.
- They do not want to learn another complex app.
- They want to hand work to an AI operator, review the result, and approve it.

## The Problem OpenShop Solves

Shopify merchants are stuck between three bad options:

1. Too many apps: translation, SEO, landing pages, creative, analytics, discounts, import tools, and automation each live in separate dashboards with separate monthly bills.
2. Too much manual work: the merchant still copies data between Shopify, spreadsheets, AI chats, design tools, ad tools, and content apps.
3. Too little real automation: most AI tools can suggest what to do, but they cannot safely act on the live store with approvals, memory, and repeatable workflows.

The real pain is not "I need another AI tool." The pain is:

- I have too many repetitive store tasks.
- I pay for too many Shopify apps.
- I do not have a team for content, ops, analytics, SEO, and ads.
- I want AI to do real work, but I need control before anything goes live.

## Core Promise

OpenShop turns store work into a conversation.

Ask for the outcome. The agent finds the right records, runs the right tools, creates the artifact, shows the result, and waits for approval before publishing.

## Product Pillars

### 1. Chat

Promise: run your store by talking to it.

What it does:

- Chat with an agent that knows products, orders, customers, and store context.
- Mention real Shopify records like products, orders, or customers.
- Ask for changes, answers, reports, rewrites, discounts, or store tasks.
- See tool calls and proposed changes before anything ships.

Problem solved:

Merchants stop jumping between Shopify admin, spreadsheets, AI tabs, and dashboards. The store becomes one conversation.

Marketing line:

Talk to your store. Approve what ships.

### 2. Studio

Promise: product photos and video from a sentence.

What it does:

- Generate product posters, hero images, UGC-style creative, and lifestyle visuals.
- Create video and ad assets from a prompt.
- Use brand memory so creative stays consistent.
- Push approved media to Shopify CDN or product pages.

Problem solved:

Small merchants cannot afford constant photoshoots, creative agencies, or ad-content production cycles.

Marketing line:

A creative team, on demand.

### 3. Autopilot

Promise: the store keeps working while the merchant sleeps.

What it does:

- Schedule recurring jobs.
- Run cloud cron tasks for restock checks, order tagging, daily reports, and alerts.
- Let the merchant choose what runs automatically and what still needs approval.

Problem solved:

Important work happens late, gets forgotten, or depends on the merchant manually checking every day.

Marketing line:

Set the job once. Wake up to the result.

### 4. Skills

Promise: teach it once, reuse forever.

What it does:

- Package workflows as reusable skills.
- Run skills manually, from chat, on a webhook, or on a schedule.
- Support built-in and merchant-authored skills.
- Improve workflows over time through outcomes and memory.

Problem solved:

Store operations live in someone's head or in SOPs nobody follows. OpenShop turns them into repeatable work.

Marketing line:

Your playbooks, run by an agent.

### 5. Analytics / Intel

Promise: ask your numbers anything.

What it does:

- Turns plain-English questions into ShopifyQL.
- Produces charts, SKU-level breakdowns, and follow-up answers.
- Connects insight directly to action: restock, campaign, discount, or task.

Problem solved:

Merchants do not want to build dashboards, write SQL, or wait for an analyst before making store decisions.

Marketing line:

Decisions, not dashboards.

### 6. Brand

Promise: build the brand once, apply it everywhere.

What it does:

- Helps define voice, story, visual direction, logo, and brand kit.
- Saves brand memory for future content, ads, product pages, and creative.
- Keeps outputs consistent across the store.

Problem solved:

AI-generated store content drifts. Every page, ad, and product description starts to sound different.

Marketing line:

One brand memory for every run.

### 7. Shopify Tools / MCP Foundation

Promise: real Shopify work, not generic AI suggestions.

What it does:

- Exposes Shopify operations as typed tools.
- Supports products, orders, customers, inventory, discounts, content, themes, analytics, webhooks, bulk operations, and more.
- Works with AI runtimes and MCP-compatible clients.
- Uses safety gates, confirmation, snapshots, and audit logs.

Problem solved:

AI usually stops at advice. OpenShop can safely act on the store.

Marketing line:

The agent has hands.

### 8. Memory

Promise: the agent gets better the more the store uses it.

What it does:

- Stores decisions, outcomes, snapshots, store patterns, and brand context.
- Learns what worked before.
- Feeds past results into future recommendations.

Problem solved:

Most AI tools forget the store between sessions. The merchant has to re-explain everything.

Marketing line:

Every run teaches the next one.

## Website Narrative

The website should tell the story in this order:

1. Pain: merchants run their store through too many apps, tabs, and manual workflows.
2. Promise: one AI operator can do the work across the store.
3. Proof: real product surfaces: Chat, Studio, Autopilot, Skills, Analytics, Brand.
4. Cost anchor: replace monthly Shopify app subscriptions and agency work.
5. Control: nothing goes live without approval.
6. Differentiator: it runs on real Shopify tools and the merchant's AI runtime.
7. CTA: connect your Shopify store and give the agent its first job.

## Messaging Rules

Say:

- AI operator for Shopify merchants
- Run your store, not your apps
- Talk to your store
- Put store work on autopilot
- Approve before it ships
- Runs on your AI keys
- Replaces the app stack

Avoid leading with:

- MCP
- 192 tools
- Protocol language
- Infrastructure language
- Generic AI assistant language
- "Chatbot"

Technical depth matters, but it should support the promise instead of becoming the promise.
